STANDALONE AUDIO / STANDALONE-AUDIOPAKETE

30 final WAV sound effects (500-529) and 19 MP3 music slots (10000-10018).
The three speech overlays are already mixed into 515, 516 and 529.
Backup and separate speech files are excluded. Numeric IDs retain their existing game mapping.

Select sound/pack.json as the sound pack and music/pack.json as the music pack
in the existing audio/data-pack settings. No new engine or simulation behavior is required.
For a portable installation, copy this directory to data/packs/standalone-audio.
