OpenSC2K Remaster graphics

Independent artwork made with OpenAI ImageGen and deterministic projection,
compositing, palette, mask and interface drawing tools. Approved Style A;
fourfold original large-view dimensions and the existing2:1 game projection.
No original SimCity2000 pixel files are included. Original metadata supplied
resource IDs, dimensions and native direction/anchor requirements.

499 city masters,112 interface masters,206 city lightmaps,293 documented unlit
masters,408 animation images and408 matching light frames,48 road masks.
1442 additional Visual artwork records:432 vehicles,96 pedestrian states,
896 woodland variants,13 ground masks and5 cloud atlases. Aliases share files.

Editable sources, prompts, masks and generators are retained in the local
Codex/standalone-data-pack/production workspace. The associated provenance

remain separate project resources with their existing license notices.
